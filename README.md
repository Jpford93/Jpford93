## About me

I'm an entrepreneur and software developer with a background in ecommerce, logistics, digital marketing, and business operations.

I've been building businesses since 2010. Across those experiences, I've designed products, developed websites, coordinated operations, and created tools that make information easier to manage.

Today, my focus is **Invenua**—bringing together what I've learned from working with physical products, people, and technology.

## What I'm building

### Invenua

**Intelligence and continuity for physical goods.**

Invenua is developing technology to help people and organizations better understand, manage, and coordinate the physical world.

Our first product, **Invenua Home**, is being prepared for external testing.
